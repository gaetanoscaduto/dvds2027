Dear colleagues,

On Thursday 17 June 2027, the Day of Sociology (Dag van de Sociologie) will take place in Tilburg. This year, the event is organised by the Department of Sociology at Tilburg University in collaboration with the Dutch Sociological Association (NSV).

The Day of Sociology brings together researchers, lecturers, students and professionals around current sociological research, with parallel panel sessions in Dutch and English, a plenary session and the presentation of the NSV awards.

Over the coming months, this website will be updated with all information on the call for panels and papers, registration, the programme and practical matters.
