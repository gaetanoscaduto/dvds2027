Call for Papers & Panels

The programme of the Day of Sociology is largely shaped by the sociological community itself. The call runs in two steps: first, panels are proposed; then, anyone can submit a paper to one of the accepted panels.

Step 1 · Panels

Propose a panel around a sociological topic. Panels can be held in Dutch or in English.

Opens: 1 December 2026
Deadline: 15 January 2027

Step 2 · Papers

Submit an abstract to one of the accepted panels. The list of panels will appear on the Sessions page.

Opens: 25 January 2027
Deadline: 19 March 2027

Submitting a panel proposal

A panel proposal includes:

  • the title of the panel;
  • name, affiliation and email address of the panel chair(s);
  • names and affiliations of the panellists (min. 3, max. 5);
  • a short description of the panel (max. 500 words);
  • the language of the panel (Dutch or English).

Panel proposals can be submitted from 1 December 2026 until 15 January 2027 via link to the form to follow.

Submitting a paper

Abstracts (max. 250 words) can be submitted from 25 January until 19 March 2027 via link to the form to follow. Please indicate which panel your abstract is intended for.

All speakers and panel chairs must also register for the Day of Sociology.
