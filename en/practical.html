Practical information

Venue

The Day of Sociology 2027 takes place on the Tilburg University campus, Warandelaan 2, 5037 AB Tilburg, the Netherlands. The building and rooms will be announced later: building to be announced.

Getting there

Take the train to Tilburg Universiteit station; the campus is a few minutes' walk from the station. It is one stop from Tilburg (central station). Plan your journey at ns.nl or 9292.nl.

More information on how to reach the campus is on the Tilburg University route page.

Food and drinks

On campus

  • Grand Café Esplanade (Esplanade building): coffee, lunch and dinner; the campus meeting place.
  • Faculty Club (Warandelaan 3): lunch and dinner on weekdays, next to the Oude Warande forest.
  • Restaurant building (building R): lunch with fresh daily dishes, including vegan options.

In town

Tilburg city centre, with many restaurants and cafés around Piusplein and Korte Heuvel, is a few minutes away by train (Tilburg Universiteit → Tilburg).
