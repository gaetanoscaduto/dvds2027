Registration

Registration form

Registration opens on 16 April 2027. You will then be able to register via link to the registration form to follow; you will be redirected to a form and payment system.

All participants (speakers, panel chairs and other attendees) must register. Participation is not possible without prior registration and payment. The registration deadline is 18 May 2027.

Fees
